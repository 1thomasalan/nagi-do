Under the hood

  1. Let iPhone zoom. The app blocks pinch-zoom today, which the accessibility check flags as a blocker.
  2. Respect Reduce Motion. The breathing orb ignores the iPhone setting; it would hold still for people who turn it on.
  3. Name every button. The settings close × has no label for VoiceOver.
  4. One type pair, from Japan. Shippori Mincho (a Japanese serif, lovely on 凪) for words and numbers, Zen Kaku Gothic New for controls, replacing three mixed fonts and nine sizes.
  5. Less glass and glow. The orb keeps its glow; buttons, the wordmark and panels drop their blur and halos so the orb owns the light.
