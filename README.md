# Nagi-do · The Way of Stillness

*Nagi (凪)* is the calm when the wind drops and the sea goes still. *Dō (道)* means "the way."

Nagi-do is a quiet meditation timer that lives on your phone. Open it, pick a time, and sit.

## Features
- Breathing orb with a soft glow that moves with your breath
- Sessions from 1 to 30 minutes, or custom up to 90
- Ending sounds: singing bowl, gong, or chime, with optional start and interval bells
- Background sounds: forest, stream, rain, ocean, or a chanted Om
- Guided breathing (Natural, 4·6, Box, 4·7·8) with an optional calm British voice guide
- A daily quote, streaks, and a 4-week journey view
- Install to your home screen; works full screen, and your data stays on your phone
